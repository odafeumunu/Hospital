# 🏥 Health Medics: Your Gateway to Quality Healthcare 🌟

A comprehensive web project designed to provide a seamless experience for patients and healthcare professionals alike. Explore our services, meet our specialists, and discover valuable resources for a healthier life!

## ✨ Project Highlights

-   **Informative Pages**: Access detailed information on various medical services and healthcare tips.
-   **Interactive Elements**: Schedule appointments and sign up for newsletters.
-   **Responsive Design**: Enjoy a consistent experience across all devices.

## 🛠️ Installation

Get started with Health Medics in just a few steps!

1.  **Clone the Repository**:

    ```bash
    git clone https://github.com/odafeumunu/Hospital.git
    ```

2.  **Navigate to the Project Directory**:
    ```bash
    cd Hospital/version
    ```
3.  **Open HTML Files**:
    Open any of the .html files in your preferred browser to view the content.

## 💻 Usage

### Navigating the Website

*   🏠 **Home**: Start your journey here for an overview of Health Medics.
*   👩‍⚕️ **Doctors**: Meet our specialists and learn about their expertise.
*   📰 **News**: Stay up-to-date with the latest health and medical news.
*   🩺 **Services**: Discover the wide range of services we offer.
*   🛍️ **Shop**: Explore health-related products in our online store.
*   🖼️ **Gallery**: Browse our photo gallery.
*   📄 **Pages**: Access additional resources and information.
*   📞 **Contact**: Reach out to us with any questions or inquiries.

### Making an Appointment

1.  Navigate to the **Appointment** page.
2.  Fill out the appointment form with your details.
3.  Submit the request and await confirmation.
